1. Who we are

SOS Emergency (“the app”) is developed by DGM Tech Products (“we”, “us”, “our”). The app lets you send an emergency alert to people you trust: it rings their phone loudly, texts them your location and calls them.

This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the SOS Emergency app for Android and Wear OS (package com.dgm.sosemergency) and to the servers that support it.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Mobile number — to create your account and sign you in with a one-time passcode (OTP). It is also how the app recognises you when someone adds you as an emergency contact.
  • Name and email address — for your profile. Your name is shown to your contacts when you send an SOS.
  • Emergency contacts — the name and phone number of each person you add, either typed in or picked from your phone’s contacts.

2.2 Information from your device, with your permission

  • Contacts. When you use “Choose from Contacts”, the app reads your phone’s contact list on your device so you can pick people. Only the contacts you select are uploaded. We do not upload, store or share the rest of your contacts.
  • Precise location. Read only at the moment you trigger an SOS, to add a Google Maps link to the text message your phone sends to your contacts. Your location is not sent to or stored on our servers, and the app does not track your location in the background.
  • Device push token. A Firebase Cloud Messaging token and your device platform, so our server can deliver SOS alarms and sign-in codes to your device. It is removed when you log out.

2.3 Information collected automatically

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics, which automatically collects information such as app usage events (for example, app opens and session length), device model, operating system and app version, approximate location at country or region level derived from your IP address, an app-instance identifier and, where available, your device’s advertising ID. We do not use this information for advertising. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not read your text messages or your call history.
  • We do not record calls or audio.
  • We do not collect your location outside of an SOS you trigger.
  • We do not collect payment information.

3. How we use your information

  • Your account — to create it, sign you in with an OTP and keep your session secure.
  • Sending your SOS — when you press SOS, the app:
    • asks our server to send an alarm to the devices of your emergency contacts who also use the app;
    • sends a text message with your location from your phone to each of your emergency contacts;
    • calls your emergency contacts from your phone, one after another.
  • Linking contacts — if you add someone who also uses the app, or they add you, your accounts are linked so that an SOS from either of you can ring the other’s device.
  • Receiving an SOS — to ring your device when one of your linked contacts sends an SOS, and to show you their name.
  • Operating and protecting the service — for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts to prevent abuse.
  • Improving the app — through the aggregated usage data described in section 2.3.

4. SMS, calls and alarms

  • SOS text messages and calls are sent from your own SIM card, only to the emergency contacts you added, and only when you trigger an SOS. Your mobile carrier’s standard charges may apply.
  • The app reads your phone’s call state only to tell when one emergency call has ended, so it can call the next contact. It does not read the numbers or history of your calls.
  • When you receive an SOS, the app may turn your alarm volume up to maximum and, if you have granted Do Not Disturb access, temporarily turn off Do Not Disturb so the alarm can be heard. Your previous settings are restored when you stop the alarm.

6. Who we share your information with

  • Your emergency contacts — this is the purpose of the app. When you send an SOS they receive your name, an alarm (if they use the app), a text message with your location and a phone call.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf to run the app: Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging, to deliver alarms and sign-in codes, and Analytics); the cloud hosting and database providers that run our server; and, where used, SMS providers that deliver sign-in codes.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

7. Storage and security

  • Information sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Sign-in codes and revoked session tokens are stored only in hashed form, and sign-in codes expire shortly after they are issued.
  • On your device, the app stores your sign-in session, your profile (name, email and mobile number) and your emergency contacts, so that SOS text messages and calls still work without an internet connection.
  • Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and emergency contacts — for as long as your account exists. Contacts you remove are deleted straight away.
  • Device push tokens — removed when you log out, and automatically once a token stops working.
  • Sign-in code records — deleted automatically, generally within a day of the code expiring.
  • Analytics data — kept by Google according to Firebase’s retention settings.

9. Your choices and rights

  • View and edit your profile and emergency contacts in the app, and remove contacts at any time.
  • Withdraw any permission in your device settings.
  • Log out to stop this device from receiving alarms.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it — see Contact us.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU General Data Protection Regulation — including the right to withdraw consent and to raise a grievance. To exercise any of them, contact us.

10. Deleting your account

To delete your account and all associated data, email dgmtechproducts@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” and your registered mobile number. After verifying the request, we will delete your account, profile, emergency contacts and device records within 30 days. Analytics data held by Google is handled according to Firebase’s retention settings.

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Not an emergency service

SOS Emergency does not contact the police, ambulance or fire services. Alerts depend on mobile networks, internet access, battery, the permissions you grant and your contacts’ devices, and may be delayed or fail. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India).

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will let you know in the app or on its store listing.
